## Open: needs data or new analysis, not done here

- The Sentinel-1 flood index peaks in the dry months (February and March are close to October) and is negatively correlated with rainfall and soil moisture on raw values. It is not shown to measure inundation, so every association built on it is uninterpretable. Validating it needs independent flood extents, which are not available.
- Dynamic World is itself negatively correlated with rainfall (its clear-image count falls from about 50 a month in the dry season to 1 to 3 in the wet season), so it cannot validate the radar index.
- The data cannot be reproduced from the repository. The v2.4.0 script is missing and "VERIFIED_GEE_EXPORT" is a string the pipeline writes about itself. The check accepts any CSV that carries that string.
- Effective sample size uses the lag-1 formula, which is too optimistic for seasonal series. The lag-1 autocorrelation of the turbidity index pairs non-adjacent months where values are missing. The multiple-testing families are narrower than the tests run, and the headline rank correlations have no confidence interval.
- Flood masks are built from 10 m radar images but evaluated at 30 to 120 m, and Zone 3 shows exactly 0 km² of permanent water although it holds the Ogun mainstem (suspected scale artefact).
- Turbidity sampling keeps only pixels classed as water, which may cut off the most turbid water.
- Rainfall and soil moisture are averaged over the whole catchment while the radar index covers only the lower floodplain below the Oyan Dam.
- The explorer's default inundation layer is a September to October 2022 composite, while the rest of the page covers 2019 to 2024.
