# Ogun River Catchment-to-Coast Environmental Observatory (2019–2024)

**Repository:** `https://github.com/oloyededavid/Ogun-Catchment-Coast-Observatory`  
**Live Interface:** `https://oloyededavid.github.io/Ogun-Catchment-Coast-Observatory/`

An integrated Earth Observation and hydrological research observatory investigating how monthly rainfall variability and shallow surface soil-moisture conditions across the Ogun River catchment relate to seasonal SAR floodplain inundation indicators and downstream estuarine spectral turbidity dynamics (`NDTI`) in the northwest Lagos Lagoon.

> **Data status: demonstration preview.** The files in `docs/data/` are placeholder values, not an authenticated Google Earth Engine export. `src/02_compile_observatory.py` flags the current time series as `DEMONSTRATION_PREVIEW`, and the website shows a warning banner while that is the case. To publish real results:
> 1. Run `gee/01_extract_real_ogun_data.js` in the Earth Engine Code Editor and download the `ogun_subbasins` and `ogun_monthly_timeseries` exports from Google Drive.
> 2. Replace `docs/data/ogun_subbasins.geojson` and `docs/data/ogun_monthly_timeseries.csv` with those exports.
> 3. Run `python src/02_compile_observatory.py` to regenerate `docs/data/cascade_diagnostics.json`. Commit its output unedited.

## 1. Research Question & Scientific Scope

**Central Research Question:**  
*How do monthly and seasonal rainfall variability and shallow surface soil-moisture conditions across the Ogun River catchment relate to SAR-detected floodplain inundation indicators and downstream estuarine spectral turbidity dynamics ($\text{NDTI}$) in the northwest Lagos Lagoon?*

### Specific Objectives
1. **Topological Catchment vs. Estuarine Separation:** Delineate the upland hydrological sub-basins of the Ogun River watershed (`WWF HydroBASINS Level 7`, Units `Z1–Z4`) along true watershed divides and separate them topologically from the downstream estuarine receiving water body in the northwest Lagos Lagoon (`Unit Z5`).
2. **Multi-Sensor Environmental Monitoring (2019–2024):** Quantify monthly CHIRPS v2.0 precipitation, ERA5-Land shallow surface soil moisture (`0–7 cm`), topographically conditioned Sentinel-1 SAR low-backscatter inundation proxy areas, and cloud-masked Sentinel-2 open-water spectral turbidity ($\text{NDTI}$).
3. **Statistical Coupling & Autocorrelation Audit:** Evaluate both raw seasonal associations and deseasonalized anomaly associations across lags $\tau \in \{0, 1, 2\}\text{ months}$ while accounting for serial autocorrelation (Bretherton effective sample size $n_{\text{eff}}$), non-normality (Shapiro–Wilk), cloud-masked missing optical months (`NaN`), and multiple testing (Benjamini–Hochberg FDR).

## 2. Study Area & Analytical Units (`EPSG:4326`)
* **Units Z1–Z4 (Hydrological Catchment Sub-Basins):** Intact `WWF/HydroSHEDS/v1/Basins/hybas_7` watershed polygons belonging to the Ogun River drainage network (`MAIN_BAS`), grouped without arbitrary latitude slicing into:
  - `Z1`: Upper Ogun Headwaters Sub-Basin (`Oyo Basement Complex`)
  - `Z2`: Oyan–Ofiki Tributary Sub-Basin (`Western Regulated Tributary`)
  - `Z3`: Middle Ogun Mainstem Sub-Basin (`Abeokuta–Papalanto–Ifo Corridor`)
  - `Z4`: Lower Ogun Alluvial Corridor (`Mowe–Isheri–Akute–Ikorodu Floodplain`)
* **2 km Riparian Corridor:** Generated by buffering `WWF/HydroSHEDS/v1/FreeFlowingRivers` reaches (`ORD_STRA >= 3`) by `2,000 m` for cloud-free land vegetation (`NDVI`) assessment.
* **Unit Z5 (Northwest Lagos Lagoon Estuarine Receiving Zone):** Topologically disjoint (`difference(catchmentGeom)`) coastal receiving domain (`[3.32°E–3.51°E, 6.45°N–6.58°N]`) where permanent open water (`JRC occurrence >= 50%`, `MNDWI > 0`, `SCL == 6`) is evaluated for spectral turbidity ($\text{NDTI}$).

## 3. Data Provenance & Processing Lineage

| Theme | Dataset (GEE Asset ID) | Resolution | Physical Variable & Processing Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sub-Basin Topology** | `WWF/HydroSHEDS/v1/Basins/hybas_7` | Vector L7 | Intact watershed polygons filtered by Ogun `MAIN_BAS`; geodesic area ($\text{km}^2$). |
| **Terrain Slope** | `COPERNICUS/DEM/GLO30` | `30 m` | Terrain slope in degrees; `slope < 5.0°` masks hillslope radar shadow from SAR proxy. |
| **Surface Water** | `JRC/GSW1_4/GlobalSurfaceWater` | `30 m` | `occurrence >= 80%` separated as permanent water; `occurrence >= 50%` masks estuarine water. |
| **Rainfall** | `UCSB-CHG/CHIRPS/PENTAD` | `0.05° (~5.5 km)` | Monthly total (`mm/month`) when $\ge 5$ pentads exist; otherwise preserved as `null`. |
| **Shallow Soil Water** | `ECMWF/ERA5_LAND/MONTHLY_AGGR` | `0.1° (~9 km)` | `volumetric_soil_water_layer_1` (`0–7 cm` surface skin layer, $\text{m}^3/\text{m}^3$). |
| **SAR Inundation Proxy** | `COPERNICUS/S1_GRD` | `10 m` (`60–120 m` zonal) | `IW`, `VV`, `DESCENDING` pass; `50 m` focal-median filter; `VV < -16.5 dB`, `slope < 5°`, `JRC < 80%`. Preserves `null` if `s1_scenes == 0`. |
| **Spectral Turbidity** | `COPERNICUS/S2_SR_HARMONIZED` | `10–30 m` | `SCL` cloud/shadow masked; restricted to open water (`SCL == 6` & `MNDWI > 0`). $\text{NDTI} = (B4 - B3)/(B4 + B3)$. Preserves `null` if `< 100` valid water pixels exist. |
